Practice areas

Three practice areas, one method.

i.

Public Law

Counsel and litigation before the State, with a strategic perspective. For companies, public bodies and individuals who need to navigate complex regulatory processes or assert their rights before the Administration.

  • Contencioso-administrativo litigation
  • Illegality claims and public-law nullity
  • Defense in sanctioning proceedings
  • Administrative appeals
  • Public procurement
  • Regulatory affairs and State relations
ii.

Immigration Law

Full support across immigration proceedings — from visa applications to defense against adverse rulings.

  • Temporary & permanent visas
  • Residency & naturalization
  • Family reunification
  • Appeals before Extranjería / DEM
  • Deportation & asylum
iii.

Human Rights

Defense of fundamental rights against the State and private actors, with a strategic and context-aware focus.

  • Constitutional protection writs
  • Complaints before INDH
  • Gender-focused matters
  • Strategic litigation
  • Counsel to organizations

Professional profile

Interdisciplinary training.
Practice with purpose.

Tamara Valeria López González is an attorney with a law degree from Universidad de Las Américas (UDLA) in Chile.

Her legal practice builds on an interdisciplinary foundation: she holds a journalism degree from the Pontifical Catholic University of Chile, postgraduate studies in communications and political science, and completed an executive leadership program at Georgetown University (Washington D.C.). Before joining the legal profession, she led strategic communications consulting teams and directed a residential center for Chile's National Service for Minors (SENAME).

Her primary focus is public-law litigation. More than a new attorney, she brings fifteen years of work in public affairs, strategic communications and the management of relationships between companies, public bodies and communities. That unusual combination — knowing that a case is also a public conversation — is the reason Humana Legal exists.

Approach

Every case is a story.
Every strategy, tailor-made.

i.

Rigorous diagnosis

We review the record in depth before proposing a strategy. Nothing worth studying is taken for granted.

ii.

Clear communication

We explain the law in human terms. You will always know where your case stands and why.

iii.

Strategic perspective

A legal matter is rarely just legal. We also assess it through the lens of communications, public affairs and institutional context.

iv.

Full confidentiality

Process

How we work, step by step.

Every engagement follows a clear method. From the first meeting and throughout the entire process, you'll always know where we are and why.

  • i. Assessment Initial session to understand facts, background, goals and constraints of the matter.
  • ii. Proposal Work plan with legal and communications strategy, timelines and fee structure.
  • iii. Execution Rigorous work, regular reports and direct communication at every milestone.
  • iv. Follow-up Final delivery, organized documentation and ongoing support tailored to the needs of the matter.
